# A global audit: components, styles, cards

Measured on 2026-08-26, on the owner's reading that "the code is written a bit
badly, there are a lot of repeated components, and some cards are underdone".
Every number below comes from a script over the source, not from an opinion.

## The cards, since that is the specific charge

**31 families. 17 have a component; 14 are assembled by hand every time.**

The 14 are not copies of one another — grouped by the parts they require, they
come out as twelve distinct recipes (Card+Table, Card+Timeline, Card+Stat,
Card+Progress, Card+Descriptions, Card+Field+Button…). Only three share a recipe,
and it is the bare `[Card]`. So "five kinds of the same card" is not what the
code shows; what it shows is a decision layer that names 31 answers and has
built 17 of them.

**6 of 31 families have a rendered specimen.** That is the sharpest measure of
"underdone" in the system: 25 families exist as a rule an agent can be told and
nobody has ever seen a picture of.

**Five card-like surfaces measured against each other:**

| pair | shared props (Jaccard) |
| --- | --- |
| ContentCard ~ ActionCard | 0.33 |
| ActionCard ~ LinkTile | 0.22 |
| ContentCard ~ LinkTile | 0.15 |
| SelectableTile ~ anything | ≤ 0.15 |
| PlanCard ~ anything | ≤ 0.13 |

Nothing here is a duplicate by contract; the highest overlap is a third, and it
is the four words every card shares (title, eyebrow, meta, actions). The feeling
that there are five of the same card is real and it is about the RENDER, not the
code: five components produce a rounded surface with a bold line at the top and
nothing on screen says which one a reader is looking at. That is a job for the
specimens above, not for a merge.

**`Card.css` is 717 lines and 83 selectors — the largest file in the package**,
larger than the entire semantic token layer (503). It carries the surface, six
media placements, three aspect ratios, two washes, a play button, a duration
plate and the rhythm rules for twenty guest components. The media half is a
different subject from the surface half.

## The styles

**267 tokens declared across three layers** (primitives 104, semantic 254,
settings 80 — semantic redeclares per theme).

**~18 are dead.** The scan first said 23; five of those (`--bp-sm` … `--bp-2xl`)
are read by the gate's breakpoint rule as TEXT rather than through `var()`, so
they are alive and the scan was wrong about them. Of the rest, the sharpest are
`--secondary` and `--secondary-hover`: a 2026-08-22 fix moved the secondary
button onto `--surface-contrast` and left both behind, declared six times across
the light, dark and media-query blocks and read by nobody.

The others: `--success-100/400`, `--warning-400/600`, `--danger-100`,
`--violet-100/300/500/800`, `--gradient-brand`, `--accent-deep`, `--font-4xl`,
`--ease-in-out`, `--sidebar-width-collapsed`, `--sidebar-drawer-width`,
`--logo-mark-height`.

**11 values carry more than one name inside a layer.** Most are coincidence and
should stay — `--icon-lg` and `--avatar-sm` are both 1.5rem because two separate
decisions landed on the same number, and this system's rule is that a token is a
decision. Three are not coincidence and should reference instead of repeat:

```
#4638d3   --brand-400  =  --series-1
#8680e7   --brand-300  =  --series-1-dark
#008300   --series-6   =  --series-6-dark
```

A chart series that IS the brand should say so, or the two drift the first time
the brand moves.

## The code

**67 exact clones, 500 duplicated lines, 1.36%.** The percentage is comfortable
and it is the wrong number to read: `package.json` records **51 clones** on
2026-08-21, and its own note says "Lower it again when the clones are folded,
never raise it to clear red". The absolute count went up by sixteen while the
denominator grew enough to hide it.

**The largest coherent cluster is one thing written seven times.** Every
floating layer declares its own surface:

| stylesheet | fixed | z-index | shadow | radius | background |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Dropdown, Popover, HoverCard, ContextMenu, CommandPalette, Combobox, Modal | each | each | each | each | each |

This is the CSS twin of the duplication fixed in JS on the same day: the
mechanism (measure, reflow, dismiss) now lives in `lib/useAnchoredLayer` and
`lib/useDismiss`, and the SURFACE those seven layers paint is still copy-pasted.
Folding it is the single highest-value change available.

The other real pairs, in order of size: `BarChart ~ LineChart` (25 lines of
chart scaffolding), `Checkbox ~ Radio` (17, the replaced native input),
`Alert ~ Toast` (16, the message surface), `Badge ~ Chip` (10, the pill).
`DatePicker ~ DateRangePicker` (15) is a recorded twin and expected.

## What the parts still do not say

**34% of components say when to reach for them** (44 of 129), up from 18% when
this was last measured, because descriptions were rewritten as parts were
touched. **13% say anything about the words that go in them** (17 of 129).

That remains the largest gap in the package, and it is the one the outside
research put first: of 158 design systems, the highest-scoring were not the ones
with the most components but the ones that documented what they had.

## Recommended, by payoff

1. **One layer surface.** A `.layer` in the utilities layer, or a token set the
   seven stylesheets share. Removes the biggest clone cluster and makes a shadow
   or radius change one edit instead of seven.
2. **Split `Card.css`.** The media half (placements, ratios, washes, the play
   plate) is its own subject and `CardMedia` is already its own part.
3. **Delete the dead tokens**, and make the three brand/series pairs reference
   rather than repeat.
4. **Specimens for the 25 card families with no picture** — the measured
   "underdone", and the thing that would answer "why are there five of these".
5. **Content guidance**, the 13%. The slowest and the one that compounds.

Not recommended: merging the five card surfaces. They measure as different and
folding them would trade five clear names for one component with thirty props.
